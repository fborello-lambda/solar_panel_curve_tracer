Quick guide

What it does

This device measures and traces the I-V curve of a small solar panel.

Safety

  • MOSFET heats up during a sweep.
  • Sweep aborts at 5 W.
  • Load capped at ~780 mA.
  • No power switch: unplug the battery.

Connecting the panel

  1. PV+
  2. PV-
  3. Power on

Measuring from the OLED

  1. MEASURE
  2. CURVE TRACER
  3. START TRACE
  4. Turn / press
  5. Chart / dynamic load

Measuring from a phone

  1. Connect to ESP32_PLOT
  2. Open http://192.168.4.1
  3. Start / Stop

What happens during a sweep

  1. Voc probe
  2. Auto-range
  3. Up to 40 points along the curve
  4. 10-15 s

Reading the result

  • Voc
  • Isc
  • MPP
  • Range and precision
  • Idle load current

Tips

  • Steady lighting
  • Don't shade the panel
  • Flicker averaged out
